# Vehicle Forensics

Vehicle forensics is a specialized branch of digital forensics focused on identifying, acquiring, preserving, analyzing, and interpreting digital evidence generated by modern vehicles and their connected ecosystems. As vehicles continue to integrate advanced infotainment systems, telematics platforms, GPS navigation, wireless communications, and mobile-device integrations, they increasingly serve as valuable sources of digital evidence.

This repository documents practical vehicle forensic investigations involving infotainment systems, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, telematics data, navigation artifacts, connected mobile devices, Bluetooth pairings, geolocation evidence, and driver activity reconstruction.

The purpose of this repository is to provide a structured collection of forensic case studies, investigative methodologies, artifact analysis techniques, and evidence-correlation workflows that can assist students, researchers, and practitioners in understanding how digital evidence can be recovered and interpreted from modern vehicle environments.

# Overview

Modern vehicles generate and store large amounts of digital information through various onboard systems. These systems may record:

- Vehicle movement and navigation activity.
- GPS coordinates and route history.
- Driver and passenger interactions.
- Mobile device connections.
- Bluetooth pairing records.
- Apple CarPlay and Android Auto activity.
- Call logs and messaging activity.
- Media playback history.
- Voice assistant interactions.
- Vehicle diagnostics and telematics data.
- Connected account information.

Vehicle forensic investigations seek to reconstruct events and identify digital evidence that may assist in criminal investigations, accident reconstruction, insurance disputes, corporate investigations, and incident response activities.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of vehicle forensic investigations include:

- Identifying connected devices and users.
- Reconstructing vehicle routes and journeys.
- Recovering navigation history and destination records.
- Examining Bluetooth and wireless pairing activity.
- Analyzing Apple CarPlay and Android Auto artifacts.
- Correlating communications with vehicle activity.
- Identifying driver behavior and interactions.
- Validating timelines through artifact correlation.
- Recovering deleted or historical vehicle-related data.
- Producing evidence suitable for forensic reporting.

# Investigation Methodology

Vehicle forensic investigations generally follow the following process:

1. Identify evidence sources.
2. Acquire forensic images or exports.
3. Verify evidence integrity through hashing.
4. Examine infotainment and telematics artifacts.
5. Analyze connected-device activity.
6. Correlate navigation and location evidence.
7. Reconstruct timelines.
8. Validate findings using multiple sources.
9. Document conclusions.
